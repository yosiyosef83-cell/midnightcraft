About

Yosef Yosef — designer, Ramat Gan.

A graphic designer originally rooted in the world of alternative aesthetics. Highly versatile and diverse work — bold, grandiose designs that step outside the conventional.

Background

Studied at Shenkar (one-year program) and spent over two years as an offline designer at Tel Aviv advertising agency Mench — working across major retail and FMCG accounts including Dor Alon, AM:PM, Aroma, PassportCard, VOYAH and ACE.

Expertise centers on keyframe creation for advertisements, extending into banners, digital posts, branding systems, merchandise for musicians, album covers and posters for events. Advertising work favours a technical approach; private clients get the full creative lead.
